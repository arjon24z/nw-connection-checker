NW-Connection Checker

Privacy Policy  |  Effective: November 11, 2026

NW-Connection Checker (the "app") lets a company confirm how its employees' computers are connected to the internet during work, and how fast the connection is. This policy explains what the app collects, where it goes, and your choices. In this policy, "the developer" means the independent developer who publishes the app: Arjon. "Your company" means the employer whose Company code you entered.

1. Who receives the information

It goes to the logs destination that your company's administrator designated (typically a Google Sheet your company controls). The developer does not receive, store or have access to this information. The app has no server operated by the developer.

2. What the app collects and sends

Collected and sent to your companyNever collected
  • Your computer's name
  • When you turn the app's switch ON and OFF, and the length of the session
  • Your connection type (Ethernet, Wi-Fi, VPN or similar) and the name of your network adapter
  • Your speed test result (download, upload, ping, jitter) and which test server was used
  • Your name or login
  • Your location
  • Your Wi-Fi network name or password
  • Your IP address (the app does not record it; see section 4)
  • Websites you visit, files, screen content or keystrokes

3. When information is sent, and your consent

  • Information is sent only while the switch is ON. Nothing is sent while it is OFF.
  • Before you turn the switch ON for the first time for a company, the app shows a notice listing what will be sent and to whom, and asks you to click I agree. If you decline, nothing is sent.
  • You can stop at any time by turning the switch OFF or uninstalling the app.

4. Speed test and third parties

Once per session the app runs a speed test against a public LibreSpeed server (the server list is fetched from librespeed.org). Like any internet connection, these servers can see your device's IP address. The app does not store or forward it. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies. Your company's Google Sheet is hosted by Google; Google's terms apply to that service.

5. How information is protected

Information is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections to the logs destination in your Company code. If you are offline, the app keeps unsent records in a small file in its local data folder and sends them later. The Company code contains an access token for your company's endpoint; keep it private.

6. Data stored on your device

Small settings, queue and log files in the app's local data folder. Uninstalling the app removes them.

7. Retention, access and deletion

Your company decides how long records are kept and who can see them. To ask what has been recorded about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, contact your company (the administrator who gave you the Company code).

8. Children

The app is intended for working adults and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
